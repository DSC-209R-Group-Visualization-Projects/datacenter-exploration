Did electricity prices rise faster in states with more data centers?

Each dot is a state. Drag a box on the left to pick states, or click a dot to add or remove one. Their price paths appear on the right next to the U.S. average.

Price is the average residential price (revenue divided by kWh, EIA Form 861) in 2025 dollars (BLS CPI-U). Data centers are operating and planned facilities from the project database, per million residents (Census 2025); the data has no build dates, so this is how many a state has now, not growth. Orange: over half of the state's residential customers are served by utilities in PJM. A state can rank high on both for other reasons (fuel costs, wildfire and storm costs, rate cases). Source: EIA-861, BLS CPI-U, Census PEP 2025, project data center database.
